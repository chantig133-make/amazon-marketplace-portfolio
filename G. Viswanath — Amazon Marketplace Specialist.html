How I run the portfolio, and how I grow it

A fixed operating rhythm, then six growth levers applied product by product.

Daily POT report

Every product, every key metric, compared with the previous day so dips surface the same morning.

Campaign review every 10 days

Flags campaigns above 70–80% ACoS and any with 10+ clicks and zero orders, so spend stays efficient.

Weekly launches, monthly lost revenue

New launches and slow movers tracked week on week; Lost Opportunities from stock-outs and blocks reviewed monthly.

Cross-functional alignment

Logistics on stock readiness for deals and launches, procurement on availability, design on packaging, imagery and A+.

1. Win the converting search term

Push the highest-intent keyword into Top of Search, and organic rank follows. Inositol to #1, NAC to Top 3.

2. Price against the category

Reprice from competitor analysis, then protect GP%. Psyllium, Bio Culture, Inositol, Astaxanthin.

3. Fix the page before buying traffic

A+ content, Q&A and comparison images built around what shoppers search. CoQ10, Magnesium, Collagen.

4. Test one variable at a time

Title A/B tests with a clear winner. "Fermentation" on CoQ10 performed ~65% better; FIP vs GDU on Bromelain.

5. Structure ads by intent

SKAGs, product targeting, broad, bid boosts on high-interest audiences, AMC. Berberine: 490 orders and €7,985 from boosted SKAGs in 3 months.

6. Time deals against competitors

Deals planned in alternate weeks by tracking competitor deal patterns, with price set from PPU and GP.
